Ramanujan's Puzzle

A certain street has between 50 and 500 houses in a row, numbered 1, 2, 3, 4, … consecutively. There is a certain house on the street such that the sum of all the house numbers to the left side of it is equal to the sum of all the house numbers to its right. Find the number of this house. This riddle was presented to Ramanujan by his friend, P. C. Mahalanobis. Ramanujan famously came up with a general solution immediately, providing a continued fraction that encoded an infinite series of solutions.
